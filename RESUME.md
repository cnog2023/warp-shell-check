## Traps a fresh session would otherwise rediscover

- WarpFactory's `null` and `weak` maps lower indices with the coordinate metric; `strong` and `dominant`
  use Minkowski (`work/matrix/verify_null_bug.py`, wiki Findings section 8). Compare against the
  dominant and strong maps, or against the consistent replicas in `exact_conditions.py`
  (`wf_observers`, `sampled_min`), never against the raw null and weak maps.
- WarpFactory's null vectors are k = (1, n)/sqrt 2, so its Null map is half the contraction at the
  k = (1, n) normalisation that the dense check and warpax's `null_deficit` use. This factor was
  mistaken for a sampling effect on 26 October. Its timelike vectors are Euclidean-normalised at ten
  speeds (`generateUniformField.m`), so the Weak and Strong maps are comparable by sign only.
- warpax's `certify_point_lmi` returns margins whose NEC, WEC and SEC values are tetrad-invariant;
  the DEC value is not (flux margin divided by `max |T_hat|`). Compare DEC by sign.
- The two outermost cells on every grid line are stencil artefacts in vacuum; drop them, as the
  paper does. The remaining noise is about 1e-7 rho0 and sits outside the shell (r = 27 m).
- The eleven shifts per compactness bracket the threshold at 0.09 / 0.10 C. A finer bracket needs
  new shift values and a new run of the `matrix` job in `.github/workflows/matlab.yml` (free, minutes
  of wall time, a new download: `work/matrix/README.md`).
- Same object, same points: the slices are the authors' construction at their settings. warpax's
  native `FuchsMetric` carries Le's own regularisation (`INDEX.md`, warpax entry) and is a second,
  separate comparison, not a substitute.
